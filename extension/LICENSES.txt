Model (model/bert-medium/)
--------------------------
Fine-tuned from ytu-ce-cosmos/turkish-medium-bert-uncased (MIT License).
Kesgin, H. T., Yuce, M. K., Amasyali, M. F. (2023). Developing and Evaluating
Tiny to Medium-Sized Turkish BERT Models. arXiv:2307.14134.
https://huggingface.co/ytu-ce-cosmos/turkish-medium-bert-uncased

Training data: OffensEval-2020 Turkish (Offensive Turkish Language Dataset),
Çağrı Çöltekin (2020), licensed under CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The model weights are an
adaptation trained on this dataset; the dataset itself is not included.
Çöltekin, Ç. (2020). A Corpus of Turkish Offensive Language on Social Media.
Proceedings of LREC 2020, pp. 6174–6184.
